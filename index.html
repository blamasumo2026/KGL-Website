About KASTASEN GHANA LIMITED

Kestasen Ghana Limited is a diversified business enterprise committed to delivering quality products, creating employment opportunities, and supporting economic growth through innovative and customer-focused business solutions.

The Company operates across multiple business sectors, including building hardware and materials wholesale, transportation services, and financial support for market women and small-scale entrepreneurs.

At Kestasen Ghana Limited, our guiding philosophy is built on excellence, affordability, opportunity, and sustainable growth.

OUR COMMITMENT

At Kestasen Ghana Limited, we believe that business is not only about making profit but also about creating opportunities, empowering people, and building a sustainable future.

Under the leadership of Mr. Emmanuel Kwesi Egyir, we remain committed to expanding our operations, serving our customers with excellence, creating jobs, and supporting the growth of businesses and communities across Ghana.

OUR BUSINESS DIVISIONS

Explore our different business divisions.

Kestasen Building Hardware & Wholesale

Kestasen Ghana Limited specializes in the wholesale distribution of a wide range of building hardware products and construction materials, including: different types and sizes of paints, paint brushes and painting accessories, padlocks, various building hardware products and materials.

We are committed to supplying quality products at competitive prices while serving retailers, contractors, builders, construction companies, and other business customers.

Our dedication to quality, affordability, and customer satisfaction makes us a trusted partner in the building and construction industry.

Kestasen Transport

Kestasen Transport is a transportation initiative operating under Kestasen Ghana Limited, established to create employement opportunities for the youth.

Through the tricycle transportation business, the company provides opportunities for young people to work, earn income and improve their livelihoods.

This initiative reflects our commitment to youth empowerment, job creation, and economic independence.

Kestasen Abrab Pa Saving

Kestasen Abrab Pa Saving is a financial empowerment initiative founded by Mr. Emmanuel Kwesi Egyir to support market women and small-scale entrepreneurs through accessible loan opportunities.

The initiative seeks to empower hardworking women, strengthen their businesses, improve their financial capacity, and promote economic independence.

Through this initiative, we believe that empowering women and supporting small businesses contributes to the development of families, communities, and the nation as a whole.
